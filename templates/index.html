                    Top Scam Playbooks & How They Work
                
      
                
      Educate yourself and your family on the exact psychology and mechanisms behind modern digital scams.
      

                
      
                    
                    
                    
      
                        
      
                            1. The Fake Electricity Board Disconnection
                        
      
                        
      
                            Tactic: Scammers send SMS claiming power will be cut tonight at 9:30 PM. They provide a fake "Electricity Officer" number.
                        
      
                        
      
                            The Trap: When panicked victims call, the scammer asks them to download AnyDesk or pay a nominal ₹10 fee via an unverified link, draining their account.
                        
      
                    
      

                    
                    
      
                        
      
                            2. The UPI "Receive Money" PIN Trick
                        
      
                        
      
                            Tactic: Posing as buyers on OLX or army officers, fraudsters offer to send an advance payment via UPI QR code or collect request.
                        
      
                        
      
                            The Trap: They instruct: "Scan this and enter your UPI PIN to claim money". Remember: You NEVER need a PIN to receive funds.
                        
      
                    
      

                    
                    
      
                        
      
                            3. Digital Arrest & Fake Police Video Calls
                        
      
                        
      
                            Tactic: Imposters pretend to be FedEx, Customs, or Mumbai Police, claiming a parcel with narcotics was found in your name.
                        
      
                        
      
                            The Trap: They place you on Skype video wearing fake uniforms and demand "security verification deposits". There is no such thing as Digital Arrest.
                        
      
                    
      

                    
                    
      
                        
      
                            4. Work-From-Home Telegram Task Scam
                        
      
                        
      
                            Tactic: Offers ₹3,000–₹8,000 daily for liking YouTube videos or giving hotel reviews.
                        
      
                        
      
                            The Trap: After paying a small initial bonus, victims are added to a Telegram group and coerced into "VIP prepaid investments" that cannot be withdrawn.
